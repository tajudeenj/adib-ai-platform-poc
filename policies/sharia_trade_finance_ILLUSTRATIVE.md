# Sharia guidance for trade finance (ILLUSTRATIVE sample, subject to the Internal Sharia Supervision Committee)

## Permissible underlying goods
In Murabaha and other sale-based financing the underlying goods must be Sharia-permissible. Financing of prohibited goods such as alcoholic beverages, pork products, gambling equipment or tobacco is not permitted and must be referred to the Sharia function.

## Ownership and possession in Murabaha
The bank must acquire ownership and bear the risk of the goods before selling them to the customer. Documents should evidence the sequence of purchase and sale.

## Role of AI in Sharia matters
AI may flag potential Sharia issues for review. AI does not issue Sharia rulings; determinations rest with the Internal Sharia Supervision Committee in line with the Higher Sharia Authority.
