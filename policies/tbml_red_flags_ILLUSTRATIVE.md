# Trade-Based Money Laundering red flags (ILLUSTRATIVE sample policy, not ADIB policy)

## Over or under invoicing
Unit price materially above or below the market range for the stated commodity may indicate value transfer through mis-pricing. Variance beyond 25 percent of the benchmark range requires investigator review.

## Vague or inconsistent goods description
Descriptions such as general goods or various items, or descriptions inconsistent with the applicant's business profile, prevent price verification and are a red flag for misrepresentation.

## High-risk jurisdictions and transshipment
Routing through high-risk jurisdictions, or multiple transshipment points without commercial rationale, increases the risk of sanctions evasion and concealment of the true destination.

## Third-party payments
Payment by a party who is not the applicant or buyer on the trade documents, without a documented reason, is a red flag for layering.

## Sanctions and watchlist matches
Any confirmed or potential match of a party or vessel against sanctions or watchlists requires the transaction to be held and escalated. The agent may recommend; only an authorised investigator may decide.
